Inventory dashboard for Malabar Spice Exports

Prepared for Joseph, Malabar Spice ExportsPrice ₹60,000Valid until 29 Oct

What I understood

Malabar Spice Exports buys pepper, cardamom and turmeric from farmers and auctions, holds it in two godowns in Mattancherry, and ships to buyers in the Gulf and Europe. Stock lives in Excel sheets that three people edit. Lots get mixed up between purchase and shipment, and low stock is noticed only when a buyer order comes in.

What you get

  • Stock by spice, grade and godown, updated as entries are made
  • Purchase entries with supplier, grade, moisture and weight, each given a lot number
  • Shipment tracking: buyer, container, port and document status, linked to lots
  • Low-stock alerts to Joseph on WhatsApp
  • Import of the current Excel sheets, 3 years of entries
  • Logins for 4 staff, with owner, godown and accounts roles

Not included

  • Tally sync or GST filing
  • A separate mobile app (the dashboard works in the phone browser)
  • Barcode scanners or weighing scale hardware
  • A portal for buyers
